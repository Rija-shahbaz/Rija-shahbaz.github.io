01 — bio

Bio

I'm a creative strategist who's spent 3.5+ years running my own practice and managing multi-client agency accounts — from nail studios to restaurants to B2B brands.

I care about the same thing every time: understanding what a specific audience is actually afraid of, or actually wants, and building content that speaks to that instead of blending into the feed. I've worked across beauty and nail art, hospitality and restaurants, home organizing and lifestyle brands, and B2B/homeware — plus a speculative SaaS campaign to stretch into tech marketing. Different rooms, same method: find the real problem, not the surface-level one, then build toward it.
