Real Client

Peara
Asian Kitchen

Turning cravings into reservations, with influencer-led storytelling instead of standard menu shots.

The Problem

Peara needed more local diners actually converting to bookings — not just followers scrolling past food photos.

The Strategy

Shifted from standard menu shots toward influencer-based and storytelling content. Built a campaign concept, "The Craving Knows," opening with a hyper-relatable hook — "Some cravings don't wait. Neither should you." — before any brand messaging, closing on a direct CTA: "What are you craving tonight?"

20-25%growth in in-person customer conversion
driven by influencer-based campaigns
Figures as reported by the client; not pulled from a formal analytics export.
