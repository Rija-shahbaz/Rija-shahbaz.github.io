Speculative — Concept Campaign

PlayPlay

A spec campaign for a video-creation SaaS — built on the insight that the real pain isn't making videos, it's waiting for them.

The Real Problem

PlayPlay's own customer stories point past "companies don't know how to make videos" toward the actual friction: companies have stories to tell, but the production pipeline — brief, agency, feedback, revisions, approval — is too slow. By the time the video ships, the moment it was meant for has already passed.

The Strategic Insight

Positioned the brand around "Your idea shouldn't wait for a production team," rather than the flatter "create videos easily." The real emotional pain isn't video-making skill — it's watching a good idea go stale while it sits in a workflow.
