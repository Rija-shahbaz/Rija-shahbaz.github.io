Speculative — Concept Pitch

Palm Beach
Organized

A real pitch for a premium home-organizing brand — lifestyle-first, not generic decluttering.

Brand Observation

Palm Beach Organized doesn't simply organize homes — they help people reduce mental clutter and create spaces that feel calm, functional, and peaceful. The social media experience should reflect that same feeling: a lifestyle brand for premium homes, not a generic home-organizing service.

The Opportunity

Since the brand's promise is decluttering, the profile itself should communicate that same sense of calm. I'd push the grid toward: more minimal, more premium, more editorial, less visually crowded, more whitespace, more lifestyle-focused — content that sells the feeling of an organized life, not just the before/after.

Added-Value Idea

Proposed a simple Content Filming Guide for clients before each shoot — camera angles, iPhone camera settings, shot lists — since most clients are experts in their business, not in creating content. Better raw footage from the client means stronger material for the creative team to work with.

This was built as a real pitch for a brand that requested a sample — it never converted into a paid engagement, so it's shown here as a concept pitch, not a managed account.
